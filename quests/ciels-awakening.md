Ciel’s Awakening restores and evolves the frozen `atlas-codex` Harvest Protocol, its Gluttony codename, App Harvest records, and Spark Catalog into a Prime-native external-intelligence lifecycle. The predecessor proves lineage only. Current merged Prime remains authoritative.

## Two-stage lifecycle

```text
HARVEST X
    ↓ read-only source verification
Harvest Record
    ↓ separately invoked
ABSORB Y
    ↓ Gluttony analysis and candidate preparation
Rimuru record or follow-on Mission
```

Harvest discovers and verifies. Absorb classifies and prepares. Neither stage grants mutation or permanence authority.
